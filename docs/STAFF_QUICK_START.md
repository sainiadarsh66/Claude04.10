# Activity Hub: Staff Quick Start

## 1. Choose who you're with
Tap the **👥 Group** button at the top and pick the resident, or "A group".

## 2. Pick an activity
Tap a big tile on the **Home** screen, then tap the activity.
Not sure what to do? Tap **🎲 Surprise me**.

## 3. Do it together
- Sit side by side so you can both see the screen.
- Tap **💡 Staff tips** for ideas on what to say.
- Change the **Level** if it feels too hard or too easy.
- Tap **🔊** to have words read aloud.
- There are no wrong answers. Just enjoy it together.

## 4. Record it
Tap **✔ Finish**, tap a smiley face, then tap **Save**.

## Handing the tablet to a resident?
Settings ⚙️ → **Turn on Resident Mode**. Tap 🔒 and enter the staff PIN to unlock.

*Works without Wi-Fi.*
